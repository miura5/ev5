Ch 9 · Cardiac output

Cardiac output is the blood the heart pumps in a minute: CO = heart rate × stroke volume. A resting adult is about 5 L/min.

  • Heart rate: set by the SA node, nudged by autonomic tone
  • Stroke volume: depends on preload, afterload and contractility
    • More preload → more stretch → bigger stroke volume (Frank–Starling)
  • Afterload rises with arterial pressure; the ventricle works harder
Read pp. 212–219
Draw the Frank–Starling curve

As the ventricle fills more, it stretches and contracts harder, so stroke volume rises. Past a point the fibres overstretch and the curve flattens; in heart failure it sits lower.
