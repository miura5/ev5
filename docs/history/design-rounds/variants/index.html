Four ways E.V. could feel. Pick one, or mix.

All four use Proprietary: your graphite, the one ember, Inter at 20/14/12, dashed suggestions, and E.V. asking for your try first. They differ in what greets you and how you move around. A is your own description, applied strictly. B, C and D are my own directions, each pushing one thing you said you're drawn to (focus, low mental load, control) further. Click any screenshot to open that mock-up and use it. Every one is clickable, and \ switches the theme.

My recommendation as lead

Start from A and borrow B's focus mode. A changes nothing in the plan's structure, so it is the safest base for cutover. B's full-window focus mode for practice and study sessions is the single idea that most protects your attention, and it is a small, self-contained addition.

After cutover, add D's objectives view (your confidence beside your accuracy) as a tab in Today. It is the strongest learning feature here, but it changes how you plan, and the plan keeps changes to your daily routine out of the switch itself. C is the most novel, but a chat-shaped home is the easiest to slide from coach to crutch, and it changes the most structure.

A · Calm desk

your description, done strictlyOpen A

The structure you've seen: a sidebar, one calm column and a detail panel, raised to your spec. Every size is 20/14/12, Ask E.V. has no chat bubbles, small key hints sit beside actions (Start next S, Commit ↵), counts are plain grey numbers, meters are the 4 px segmented ones, there's a crisp focus ring and 150–250 ms fades. Ember appears only on the one action and E.V.'s mark.

Good for

  • Seeing your whole day and your exams at a glance
  • Familiarity: the same places as ev4, only calmer

Watch out

  • The least new; Today still lists everything
  • Key hints add small marks to the screen

Plan impact

  • Key hints may sit beside actions (UI rule 8)
  • Two-line rows are 48 px
  • No change to structure or scope

B · One thing now

focus first · my directionOpen B

There's no sidebar. Home is Now: one quiet statement of the next block with one Start, then the rest of today as your list to reorder. Starting a block fades everything away, leaving just the question or the note, a thin timer line, and E.V.'s checks one at a time below the work. ⌘K is how you get around.

Good for

  • Starting without deciding: beats putting it off
  • Retrieval with nothing else in view

Watch out

  • Less overview; exams and weak spots are a tab away
  • Progress and Activity are only reached by ⌘K or the bar

Plan impact

  • The sidebar becomes a top bar, plus a new focus layer
  • The detail panel becomes a full page
  • Today is renamed Now; same folders and scope

C · The thread

Slack-led · my directionOpen C

Today is one calm thread per day with E.V. She posts the plan in the morning, and the day collects quiet lines: "Review set done · 6 of 8 · 2 misses → weak spots", "Mock exam is writing… 9 of 15". Practice questions appear inline, one at a time. Each course is a channel. You ask E.V. right in the thread.

Good for

  • One place tells the day's story
  • You watch misses turn into weak spots

Watch out

  • A chat-shaped home invites "just answer me"
  • The plan scrolls out of view; long sets feel cramped inline

Plan impact

  • Adds a courses area; Ask E.V. merges into the thread
  • New rule: one current E.V. line per screen
  • The most structural change of the four

D · The runway

exam-led, built for self-assessment · my directionOpen D

Home is the days from today to your next exam. Today is open with one Start; future days are one line each, so you see topics come back. Objectives shows your own confidence (Low / Some / Sure) beside your actual tries, with a quiet "overconfident" where they disagree. You rate first, E.V. proposes blocks into the days, and you move them.

Good for

  • Seeing spacing: topics recur across days
  • Training your judgement of what you know

Watch out

  • Everything leans on an exam date
  • Calibration can sting; it's kept to quiet grey words

Plan impact

  • Today becomes Runway; Progress folds into it
  • Brings calibration and you-first planning into cutover (your call)
  • One new field, additive
