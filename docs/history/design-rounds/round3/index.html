Four balanced E.V.s, between Linear and Quizlet

All four use one plain, readable sans-serif (Inter, or Figtree in L), start from A's spacing and put your courses in the sidebar as C did. Each solves the "sea of rows" problem with an object language: a note looks like a note, a flashcard set like a deck, a mock exam like a timed paper, a procedure like numbered steps, a job like E.V. at work, and a plan item like a detailed, editable Linear issue. They differ in where they sit between the two axes you named.

Linear · Slack · Quip (productivity)Quizlet · Duolingo · Airbnb (learning, consumer)
I · Workbench K · Campus J · Studio L · Study hall

How to judge: open each one and spend a minute on Today, then the Library and a set page, then Notes. Ask yourself whether you can tell what everything is at a glance, and whether it still feels calm. Every direction is clickable, \ switches its theme, and the narrow screenshot on the right of each row is the phone Library.

My recommendation as lead

K · Campus, with L's Library home. K is the one most like A, which you liked most: dark, calm, the same spacing. It makes your courses real places you add and track, as C's sidebar hinted, each with its own Overview, Plan, Library, Notes and Exams. Its object language is clear without extra colour, and its notes are the most Quip-like. L's Library home is the best answer to "the phone will centre on the Library": one big Continue studying card, Due today, then sets by type. Putting that home into K gives you a desktop that runs the semester and a Library that's ready for the phone.

J is the pick if you'd rather have Airbnb's light, modern warmth and course covers; it's the most polished-looking. I is the safest (A, with real depth), but it may still feel too close to what you called shallow. L on its own leans furthest towards a learning app; its planning screens are quieter, which is fine if the Library matters most to you.

I · Workbench

about 70% productivityA, with real depth and distinct objectsOpen I

A's dark graphite, Inter and layout. Plan items are Linear issues grouped by morning, afternoon and evening, each with chips. Every type gets a small tinted type tile, used in lists, sets, source links and search. The Library is a grid of tiles whose insides tell the type: a deck's first term, a practice set's right/wrong pips, a mock exam's questions, minutes and score, a procedure's first steps.

K · Campus

about 60% productivityorganized around your courses, like Slack around channelsOpen K

The sidebar has Today, Ask E.V. and Activity, then Courses (with "+ Add course"), then All courses (Library, Notes). Each course is a space with tabs: Overview · Plan · Library · Notes · Exams. A's dark calm, slightly warmer and rounder. Shape says what a thing is and colour says which course: a deck with a stacked edge, practice pips, a mock exam with a stopwatch and a perforated foot, a procedure on a numbered rail, a note as a folded page.

Good for

Plan impact

J · Studio

the midpointmodern like Airbnb, organized like Linear and SlackOpen J

Light first: warm white, soft rounded cards where you browse, rows where you scan, and one terracotta accent. Courses get calm patterned covers, like Airbnb listings. Plan items show their properties as chips under the title. Set tiles each carry their type: a deck with peeking card edges, practice pips, a mock exam "ticket" with a perforation, a procedure's numbered ladder. A job is its own darker "E.V. is working" card.

L · Study hall

about 70% learninga study app you'd open on the busOpen L

The Library is the heart: one Continue studying card with a Resume button, Due today, then sets by type, each with its own muted tint and tile art (flashcards teal, practice violet, mock exams berry, procedures olive). The set page is Quizlet's: mastery, four study modes, a big card and the term list. Study modes fill the screen. Today, notes and plan items stay quiet and Linear-like. Figtree on warm paper, no gamification.
