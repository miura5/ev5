E.V. v5 · design directions E–H · synthetic data

Four different E.V.s. Which one feels like yours?

Each direction has its own design system (colour, type, shape, motion, components) and its own personality. All four aim at your words: calm, restrained, one accent, flat, keyboard-first, focus on one thing. They just get there differently. All four keep the coach rules: you try first, every fact is cited, and E.V. proposes while you decide. E and F follow your priorities (a Quizlet-style Library, the Peter Parker feel). G and H are my own proposals.

How to judge: open each one cold and spend a minute on Today, then the flashcards. Notice which one you want to study in on a tired Tuesday night. Every direction is clickable, \ switches its theme, and each has a design-system page (the last screenshot in each row).

My recommendation as lead

E · Darkroom, unless F or G grabs you at first sight. E is the closest to everything you've written (dark first, one warm accent, monochrome, precise), and it adds the personality A–D lacked. The personality comes from layout and captions, not decoration, so it should age well. Its Library (a contact sheet, then one card under a loupe) gives you the Quizlet flow without becoming carousel-driven, and it changes the plan's tokens, not its structure.

F is the warmest and the most "study tool"; pick it if light paper and a real card in your hand feels right. G is the calmest to read and the one most likely to look as good in ten years; pick it if you'd rather study on a page than in an app. H is the fastest, but you'd type commands every day; I'd only pick it if that thrills you.

Tell me a letter, or a letter plus something you liked elsewhere (for example "E, with F's card flip"). That direction's system becomes Proprietary in kit/, its mock-up becomes design/mockup.html, and I'll update the plan to match. One scope note: every direction's Library uses flashcard decks, and in the plan cards come after the switch (C3). Choosing any of these, I'd still bring decks right after the switch, with the practice part of the Library ready at the switch itself.

E · Darkroom

your prioritiesa photographer's darkroom and contact sheetOpen E

Pure neutral black · one safelight orange-red · Schibsted Grotesk with IBM Plex Mono "film-edge" captions (FR 03 / 12). Status is shown by how filled a mark is (ring, half, dot), never by colour. Keyboard focus is four crop marks, and E.V.'s mark is a registration mark. The light theme is "Print".

Feels like

  • Precise, nocturnal, focused
  • A tool a science-and-photo nerd built for himself

Good for

  • Late-night study without glare
  • A deck you can see at a glance, studied one frame at a time

Watch out

  • Pure black is stark in daylight (Print theme covers it)
  • Mono captions must stay sparse

Plan impact

  • New tokens: safelight instead of orange, two new fonts
  • One extra size (28) for the loupe card
  • No structure change

F · Index

your prioritiesthe index card, on a warm paper deskOpen F

Warm paper, light first · fountain-pen blue ink as the only colour · Newsreader serif on cards and in E.V.'s voice, Source Sans 3 for the interface. A 5:3 ruled index card is the signature object, with one card in focus per screen. The dark theme is "Desk lamp".

Feels like

  • Warm, tactile, honest
  • Your own stack of cards, made beautiful

Good for

  • The closest to Quizlet's flow, but calm
  • Writing your answer before the card turns

Watch out

  • Light first, a change from your dark preference
  • Long answers need a pattern beyond 5:3

Plan impact

  • Top bar instead of a sidebar
  • Sizes 26 and 16 inside cards (an exception to the 3-size rule)
  • Same six areas

G · Margins

my proposala finely set textbook page; E.V. in the marginOpen G

Warm paper · one rubrication red · Source Serif 4 with oldstyle figures and small caps. E.V. speaks as numbered sidenotes beside what she means. Data shows as word-sized sparklines. Practice is set like a workbook exercise, and flashcards turn like leaves. The dark theme is "Night reading".

Feels like

  • Quiet, scholarly, timeless
  • A textbook that talks back

Good for

  • Long reading and note-taking
  • Never covering your work: E.V. sits beside it

Watch out

  • Light first, and serif throughout
  • Needs a wide screen (about 1100 px)

Plan impact

  • The Detail panel becomes the margin
  • New tokens (red, serif, 23/17/13)
  • Progress folds into Library and Today

H · Console

my proposala calm command surfaceOpen H

Soft blue-black · one warm cursor accent · IBM Plex Mono for structure, Plex Sans for reading. One column of output and one command line: type today, cards nur112, plan exam3, or pick from completions. Status is a glyph's shape (○ ◐ ● ◆). The light theme is "Paper terminal".

Feels like

  • Fast, intimate, nerdy in a good way
  • Peter Parker's own code

Good for

  • Never touching the mouse
  • One command registry drives everything

Watch out

  • Typing commands every day isn't for everyone
  • The least glanceable

Plan impact

  • A console shell replaces the sidebar and Detail panel
  • Areas register commands, not nav items
  • Keyboard paths in every e2e test
