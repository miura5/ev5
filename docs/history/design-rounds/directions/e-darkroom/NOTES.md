# Direction E · Darkroom

**In one line:** E.V. as a photographer's darkroom and contact sheet: one neutral-black ground, one safelight, fine film-edge captions, and a loupe that holds a single card.

**The system ("Proprietary · Darkroom")**
- **Palette:** a ground of `#0A0A0A`, hairlines `#1F1F1F` and `#2E2E2E`, and four inks (`#E6E5E1`, `#A3A19C`, `#8A8884` for captions, `#3D3C3A` for ticks only). The safelight `#E8613C` is a warm red-orange (5.9:1 on the ground), used only on the one primary action and on E.V.'s mark where she speaks.
- **Status by density, not hue:** an open ring for weak, a half dot for shaky, a filled dot for solid, plus running, done and failed marks.
- **Print theme (`\`):** paper `#F3F1EC`, ink `#1A1917` and a deepened safelight `#B5401C`. All text is at least 4.5:1 in both themes.
- **Type:** Schibsted Grotesk for content and IBM Plex Mono for captions. Sizes are 20, 14 and 12 mono, plus 28 only under the loupe. Weight 500 is used only for titles and the primary label. Captions are in sentence case: "FR 03 / 12", "Ch 9 notes · ¶ 3", "07:42 CT".
- **Shape:** square prints. Controls have a 2 px radius, frames have 0, and every line is a 1 px hairline. Depth is flat: only ⌘K and the toast cast a shadow. E.V.'s proposals are dashed.
- **Motion:** one curve, (.2, 0, 0, 1), at three durations: shutter 120 ms, advance 200 ms and develop 360 ms (the back of a card fades in like a print developing). Reduced motion turns all of it off.
- **Signature components:** the contact sheet (each deck a filmstrip; the deck that's due sits on the light table with numbered frames), the loupe, frame ticks, **crop-mark focus** (four corner marks, no glow ring), captioned prints, and E.V.'s **registration mark**: where she is, things line up.

**Why it fits Matt.** It is monochrome plus one accent, taken literally: each screen has exactly one bright thing, and it is the next step. Mono captions with times, frame numbers and sources read like lab equipment, which earns trust. It feels like a science student's own tool, made by someone who also develops his own film: exact, never corporate. The metaphor lives in layout and captions, never in texture.

**The carousel without being "carousel-driven"**
- The deck overview is an index of tiny frames, not slides.
- Studying puts one card under the loupe. The strip shrinks to 1 px ticks, with the current one tall, and the neighbours show only as two faint edges, "FR 02" and "FR 04".
- ←/→ moves frame. He types or says his attempt first, then flips with Space (↵ from the field).
- The back develops in place: the cited answer, his attempt quoted, one line from E.V. Again (1) and Good (2) leave a ring or a dot on the tick, and the sidebar dims while the loupe is up.

**A day in it**
- **07:42:** He presses ↵ on "Start morning review" and the cards come one at a time under the loupe.
- **12:10:** He opens ⌘K, chooses oral liquid doses, picks with 1–4, commits with ↵, and the cited feedback develops in.
- **Afternoon:** Activity says "Needs you". He rates the four objectives, the plan uncovers, and he approves it with ⌘↵.
- **Evening:** Ch 9 notes, in a session with a timer, read-along and "Saved on this device", plus E.V.'s check question answered in his own words.

**Risks:** long reading on black (Print is one key away); density-only status needs its small legend; metaphor words (contact sheet, frame) could feel cute, so job states and actions stay plain; 28 px is a fourth size. **In five years:** black, hairlines, a grotesk and a mono, with no gradients, glass or grain. It should age like a camera manual.
