Cardiac output

Cardiac output is the volume the heart pumps each minute: CO = HR × SV. At rest it is about 5 L/min.

Frank–Starling

The more the ventricle fills, the harder it contracts, up to a point. More stretch means more overlap between actin and myosin.

  • Preload: stretch at the end of diastole (EDV)
  • Afterload: the pressure the ventricle pushes against
  • Contractility: force at a given stretch
